About the role Sika waterproofing sheets have proven themselves for many decades worldwide on different roof structures. Our systems “Made in Switzerland” seal roofs sustainably and reliably. Various accessories and the corresponding thermal insulation complement the offer. Whether green, bekiest or without protective and useful layer – we cover the entire spectrum. For our internal sales service in Sarnen/OW we are looking for a new team member.

In this role, you are responsible for the following tasks

  • Sales from order to delivery
  • Treatment of all customer orders including credit, debit and returns
  • Monitoring delivery dates, preparing customer dossiers and invoicing and processing of residues
  • Organising transport and returns
  • Processing of transit transactions
  • Supervision of field workers in the assigned sales region
  • Support and participate in the continuous improvement process

Your skills and experience

  • Basic commercial training completed
  • Several years of professional experience in internal sales as well as in-depth SAP knowledge in the SD module
  • Very good PC knowledge (Outlook, Word & Excel)
  • Joy of communication, German and French on the phone
  • High self-motivation and pleasure in customer contact
  • Reliable, structured, exact and independent working method

Why you should come to us

  • Trust is the most important thing for us. We trust in your skills and provide you with many design spaces – every day anew
  • A 40-hour week
  • Generous training offers
  • Attractive Preventive Solution
  • Free parking in front of the house

Sika is a great employer – especially for employees who rely on trust in cooperation. We welcome your complete application dossier (CV, certificates, diplomas, etc.).

We offer competitive remuneration based on local market standards and the specific responsibilities of each job. The remuneration shall be based on the skills, responsibility, education and/or training relevant to the position. We are committed to fair and equitable remuneration schemes in accordance with applicable laws, the Chemicals Tariff Agreement and regulations.

About Sika Sika is a specialty chemical company, global leader in the development and production of systems and products for gluing, sealing, steaming, reinforcing and protecting in construction and industry. Sika has a global presence with subsidiaries in 103 countries, produces in over 400 factories, develops innovative technologies for customers around the world and thus contributes significantly to the sustainable transformation in construction and transport. The more than 33,000 employees generated a turnover of CHF 11.20 billion in 2025.
