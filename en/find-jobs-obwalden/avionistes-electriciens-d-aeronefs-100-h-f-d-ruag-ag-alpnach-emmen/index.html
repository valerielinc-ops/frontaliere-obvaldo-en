Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organizations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • Working on helicopter avionics systems
  • Performing inspections of electrical and electronic systems

  • Analyze and resolve disruptions

You bring that with you

  • EASA B2 license or experience as an avionician
  • Good German and English skills (B2 level)
  • Enthusiasm for Aviation
  • Readiness for continuous training
  • High motivation and strong team skills

Do you not meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. Place of work

RUAG AG, Industriestrasse 5, 6055 Alpnach

Over the area

We are the helicopter competence center of the Federal Department of Defence, Civil Protection and Sport (VBS) and certified service center for Airbus Helicopters. We handle both military and civilian helicopters.

Our responsibility is to return the helicopters safely, inspected and reliably to our customers after the inspection. In addition, we carry out troubleshooting both at the RUAG Competence Center and directly at the customer’s site. Modifications are also part of our tasks – from the simple conversion of radio or navigation systems to extensive cockpit adaptations.
