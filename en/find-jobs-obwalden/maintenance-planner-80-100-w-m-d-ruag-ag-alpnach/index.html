Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organizations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • Control and coordinate production and maintenance planning
  • Preparation of production and maintenance contracts
  • Coordinating material procurement and disposition
  • Support in the rough and fine planning of helicopter downtimes (maintenance)
  • Organising the conclusion of maintenance contracts and their archiving
  • Link between Production, Configuration Management, Continuing Airworthiness, Supply Chain Management, Item Management, Engineering and Order Management

You bring that with you

  • Technical training in the industrial environment as a polymechanic, mechanic, plant/device builder, spengler or the like, further training as a process specialist a plus
  • Experience in aircraft maintenance and work preparation is beneficial
  • Secure handling of MS-Office and SAP desirable
  • Very good German and good English skills in word and writing
  • Dutiful, self-employed and responsible work
  • Team-oriented and flexible personality with strong communication and cooperation skills

Do you not meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all individuals, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a job in an army-related environment to apply.
