Full description

Facing the challenges of our time Help us grow and be more impactful! We are looking for a passionate intern - starting date ideally in January 2027

Your mission

As an intern in the Automated Sample Handling group, you will support all activities in the Life Science Technologies area with a focus on lab automation, robotics, liquid handling, and consumable development for bioprocessing and chemical applications. This position will involve developing and testing new consumables for robotic lab automation systems. Within the strategic goals of the group, you will contribute to the development of disruptive technologies. The work will be primarily engineering, but in close collaboration with biologists, chemists, and physicists (may involve travel to other CSEM sites).

Your responsibilities

  • Design, fabrication / prototyping, and assembly of prototype systems
  • Performing of validation experiments for prototype systems
  • Analysis of resultant data and preparation of presentations summarizing the results

Your profile Know-how

  • Strong background in a life science-related field (e.g. engineering, physics, biology, chemistry)
  • Wet lab experience, cell culture experience is a plus
  • Experience with robotic lab automation, ideally programming of liquid handling robots (OpenTrons, Hamilton, Tecan, etc.)
  • Rapid prototyping (e.g. 3D printing, micromilling, hot embossing), CAD (e.g. SolidWorks)
  • Word, Excel, PowerPoint
  • Strong English-speaking skills required, German and / or French-speaking skills a plus

Interpersonal skills

  • Driven and motivated
  • Good communication skills
  • Highly collaborative
  • Flexible and adaptable thinking / operating style
