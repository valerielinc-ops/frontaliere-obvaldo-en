Become part of our team!

At Raiffeisenbank Obwalden, the personality at the reception creates an inviting atmosphere with warmth and attention, in which the customer feels welcome. As the first point of contact, it significantly shapes the bank’s appearance and conveys its trustworthiness and partnership in daily contact.

What do you expect?

  • Reception of customers and ensuring a professional process in the customer zone.
  • Support for customers directly at the counter for issues such as e-banking, TWINT, cards or digital processes.
  • Recognition of customer needs as well as appointment agreement and placement of customers specifically to the responsible consultants.
  • Processing of customer mutations such as address and name changes or powers of attorney, orders and general administrative tasks.
  • Responsibility for the management, coordination and supply of cash devices as well as ATMs.

What do you bring?

  • Successfully completed training in the banking industry, catering/hotel industry or in the retail trade with the willingness to learn new topics of the banking world and to train accordingly.
  • Delight in customer contact and enthusiasm through an open, service-oriented and winning way.
  • Interest in financial services and digital solutions as well as motivation to familiarize yourself with new tasks.
  • Initiative, reliability and a sense of responsibility – combined with the pleasure of working together in a team.
  • A connection to the region and proximity to the local people round off the profile.
