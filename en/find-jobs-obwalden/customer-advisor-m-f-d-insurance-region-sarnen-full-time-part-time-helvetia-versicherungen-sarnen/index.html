Setting new standards together and shaping the future – for us, that means more than just ticking off tasks. What you achieve

  • Always keeping your finger on the pulse: Through our basic training and continuous further education, you stay up to date professionally and offer the highest level of consulting competence.

What you bring

  • Experience & expertise: You bring experience in client consulting or sales – ideally in the financial or insurance industry. Ambitious career changers who are ready to hit the ground running are also very welcome.

  • Service is your strength: Field service is your passion, and your high customer orientation and service readiness distinguish you.
  • Commitment & personal initiative: With above-average motivation and entrepreneurial spirit, you are ready to achieve great things and exceed goals.
  • Regional anchoring: Your place of residence is in the Sarnen region, where you look after your clients. You have a strong network in the region and appreciate building and maintaining new contacts.
