Full description

Around 3000 RUAG employees make a significant contribution to Switzerland's security every day. As Team Leader Mechanics Super Puma, you are responsible for the technical and personnel management of the mechanics team in the Base Maintenance area. You ensure that all maintenance, inspection and modification work is carried out efficiently, on schedule and in full compliance with EASA Part-145 and the internal MOE regulations. In close cooperation with Avionic, Sheet Metal, CAMO and Quality, you ensure smooth, safe and high-performing maintenance processes.

What you can achieve

  • Team leadership: Leading, scheduling and assigning work to the Super Puma mechanics team
  • Maintenance & compliance: Ensuring that maintenance and modification work is carried out on schedule, safely and in compliance with regulations
  • Coordination: Interface management with Avionic, Sheet Metal, CAMO, Quality, customers and management
  • Process & performance: Optimizing workflows, resource planning, capacity management and reporting
  • Safety & quality: Promoting an active safety and quality culture within the team
  • Development & capacity: Planning further development, ratings and a sustainable team and resource strategy

What you bring

  • Several years of professional experience in helicopter maintenance or Continuing Airworthiness; project management experience in a technical or flight operations environment is a plus
  • Proven leadership experience in a technical or flight operations environment, managing at least 5 employees, ideally with further training in CAS or Human Factors
  • Practical knowledge of EASA Part-145 regulations, an EASA Part-66 B1/B2 license (helicopter) and Type Rating H215 (AS332 Super Puma) are an advantage
  • Very good German and English skills, both spoken and written
  • A structured, responsible and safety-oriented approach to work, a highly resilient personality and decisiveness in stressful maintenance situations
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills, e.g. successfully coordinating interdisciplinary teams

Do you not meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We place great importance on diversity and equal opportunities, and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all people, regardless of their gender, who may have reservations about accepting a position in a military-related environment, to apply.

About the department

As a helicopter center of competence and certified service center of Airbus Helicopters, we service military and civilian helicopters with the highest precision. Our aim: to return helicopters to our customers after every inspection safe, checked and ready for use. We also rectify faults efficiently – both at our center of competence and directly on site at the customer's premises. Safety, quality and operational readiness are our top priorities.
