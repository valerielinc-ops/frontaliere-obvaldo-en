Full description

The Cantonal Hospital Obwalden is an innovative, versatile primary care hospital in central Switzerland with the clinics general, accident and visceral surgery, orthopaedics, internal medicine, ENT, urology, ophthalmology, spinal surgery, gynecology and obstetrics as well as anesthesia.

Are you ready to develop yourself, break new ground and are you looking for a varied new challenge? Then we want to get to know you.

As of 1 August 2027, we will award an interesting and varied apprenticeship as Cook / Chef EFZ

Your tasks

  • Cooperation in the preparation of hot and cold food for patients, employees and external guests
  • Checking orders and quality of food
  • Use of various cooking techniques, including cooking, saucing, steaming, stewing, gratinating and frying
  • Compliance with specifications, processes, hygiene and safety standards
  • Short internships in other departments

Your profile

  • You have completed compulsory schooling
  • You enjoy working with food
  • You excel in your resilient and creative way
  • You like working in a team

Our offer

  • A workplace in an attractive location in Sarnen by the lake, 5 minutes walk from Sarnen station
  • Multifaceted, process-oriented and interdisciplinary organization
  • discounted parking and canteen food, worldwide private accident insurance, free fitness subscription in the in-house physical fitness and discounted Reka-Card
  • An interesting, varied task in a family, small hospital with short decision-making paths and lived du-culture
