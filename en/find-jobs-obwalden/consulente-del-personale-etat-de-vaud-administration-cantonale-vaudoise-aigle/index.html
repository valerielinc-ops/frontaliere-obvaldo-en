Full description

The Regional Employment Offices (ORP) in the canton of Vaud, attached to the Directorate-General for Employment and Labour Market (DGEM), are responsible for assisting clients in their job search and supporting employers in the recruitment of staff. They play a key role in the implementation of employment policy and the fight against unemployment. As a staff adviser, you work in support of the integration of persons seeking employment. From this perspective, you follow and advise each client of the Public Employment Service, with the aim of its rapid and sustainable placement in the labour market. You select the appropriate placement strategies and have specific measures to develop their employability for each. You also guarantee compliance with the legal requirements for persons within your portfolio. Thanks to the quality and relevance of your candidate proposals, you are a privileged partner of companies in their search for staff. You develop and maintain relationships with companies in the regional economic fabric, present them with the services and measures offered by the ORPs and identify new investment opportunities for clients.

  • Establish a reintegration strategy for each client in a personalized and always tailored manner to their needs and profile;
  • Enable and advise each client through counselling and follow-up interviews;
  • To carry out professional and development reviews of the professional and personal skills of persons within its portfolio through the provision of further training and training measures;
  • Actively support the proposal of vacancies for public employment service clients;
  • Ensure, in accordance with federal and cantonal laws on unemployment insurance, compliance with obligations and suitability for placement of users;
  • Carry out a permanent update of the computer database (Plasta) and the day-to-day management of administrative tasks for each client;
  • Strengthen collaboration with the various interinstitutional partners (Social Services, Disability Insurance Office, etc.);
  • Develop and maintain contacts with enterprises in the regional economic fabric in order to promote the benefits and measures of the ORPs and to identify investment opportunities for clients of the Public Employment Service. For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact Mr Attila Molnar, HR Manager, at 021 316 62 93.
