Full description

The Cantonal Hospital Obwalden is an innovative, versatile primary care hospital in central Switzerland with the clinics general, accident and visceral surgery, orthopaedics, internal medicine, ENT, urology, ophthalmology, spinal surgery, gynecology and obstetrics as well as anesthesia. Are you ready to develop yourself, to break new ground and are you looking for a varied new challenge? Then we want to get to know you.

As of 1 August 2027 we will award an interesting and varied Apprenticeship as a merchant / merchant EFZ (a)

Your tasks

  • Collaboration in the patient administration department, where you work in the billing system of our hospital
  • Participation in financial accounting, where you will be introduced to accounting
  • Work in human resources, where you create employment contracts and process payroll
  • Short internships in other specialist departments of the hospital

Your profile

  • You have completed compulsory schooling
  • You have a flair for numbers
  • Your knowledge of German is very good and you can express yourself well orally and in writing
  • Working in a team makes you happy
  • You are reliable, can work independently and have an exact working method

Our offer

  • A workplace in an attractive location in Sarnen by the lake, 5 minutes walk from Sarnen station
  • A versatile and exciting education accompanied by experienced vocational trainers
  • Good employment conditions such as reduced canteen food, worldwide private accident insurance, free fitness subscription in the in-house physio-fitness and reduced Reka-Card
