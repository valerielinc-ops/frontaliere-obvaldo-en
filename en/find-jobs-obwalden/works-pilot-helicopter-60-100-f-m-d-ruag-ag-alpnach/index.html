Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organisations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • Maintenance Check Flights (MCF) on Super Puma/Cougar, EC635 of the Swiss Air Force
  • After acquiring the civilian AS332 single pilot rating: technical flights on aircraft of military or civilian customers at home and abroad
  • Participation in RUAG engineering and marketing projects
  • Technical editing of pilot documentation
  • Working as technical expert in the event of accidents
  • Later training and obtaining an EASA CAT 2 flight test rating (H)

You bring that with you

  • Swiss military pilot (military pilot or professional military pilot)
  • AS332/532 rating of the Swiss Air Force mandatory, EC635 rating desirable
  • To complement our existing team, we are looking for a young team member. Therefore, several years of flight experience on the Super Puma are already sufficient
  • CPL/IR with ATPL(H)
  • Pronounced technical interest, high commitment, flexibility and service and customer orientation
  • Team player (with proven leadership skills of advantage, independent working methods and entrepreneurial thinking)
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
  • Good Office Software Knowledge
