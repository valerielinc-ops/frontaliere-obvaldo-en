Dipl. paramedic HF with specialist responsibility (a) 80 - 100% As of January 1, 2027 or by arrangement

The emergency service of the Obwalden Cantonal Hospital ensures pre-hospital emergency care in the Canton of Obwalden around the clock. We combine a high level of professional expertise with a spirit of innovation and the aim of continuously developing preclinical care together with our partners. As a committed and future-oriented rescue service in Central Switzerland, we create space for new ideas, modern structures and sustainable solutions. To strengthen our operational management team, we are looking for specialists who want to take on responsibility, contribute their experience, actively promote developments and shape the future of our rescue service together with our team.

Choose your focus As a specialist, you not only work in the system, but also actively shape the system. You are given space to initiate innovations, implement new ideas and create sustainable improvements together as a team.

  1. Education
  • You create a modern, high-quality education for our students.
  • You coordinate needs-based further education and training in the Central Switzerland Education Association.
  • You promote the exchange of knowledge and professional development within the team.
  • You continually develop digital learning offerings and training tools.
  1. Infrastructure and logistics
  • You will proactively develop our infrastructure, logistics and medical technology.
  • You ensure the availability of vehicles, materials and technical systems.
  • You coordinate maintenance, service work and material management.
  • You support procurement projects and drive technical innovations.
  1. Planning and deployment
  • They connect people, resources and partners to form a powerful overall system.
  • You will further develop our service and operational planning in a future-oriented manner.
  • You strengthen cooperation with our partner organizations.
  • You design concepts for major events, MANV and KATAMED situations.
  1. Quality & process management
  • They make quality visible and drive improvements continuously and sustainably.
  • You will further develop our quality, data and process management.
  • You analyze usage and quality data and derive concrete improvements from it.
  • You design and optimize our process landscape.

This is what you bring with you

  • Diploma as paramedic HF or transport paramedic FA
  • Several years of experience in the Swiss rescue system
  • Interest in organizational development and innovation
  • A structured and solution-oriented way of working
  • What is important is not that you master every task perfectly, but that you take responsibility, recognize opportunities and actively promote developments.

You're right for us if you...

  • Take responsibility, work independently and goal-oriented and demonstrate organizational skills.
  • Enjoy working with people, communicating openly, sharing knowledge and promoting constructive collaboration.
  • Question what already exists, think in a solution-oriented manner and actively initiate improvements and changes.
  • Develop together, design new solutions and help shape our rescue service with openness, commitment and initiative.

This is something you can look forward to

  • Creative scope and responsibility: You take responsibility for an important department and actively shape its further development with a visible impact.
  • Meaningful projects: Work on projects that create sustainable added value for the population and the emergency services.
  • Development opportunities: We support your personal and professional development and specifically promote further training.
  • Modern work culture: You can look forward to short decision-making processes, flat hierarchies and a committed team that lives trust, appreciation and cooperative cooperation.

Would you like to contribute your practical experience and at the same time help shape the future of our rescue service?
